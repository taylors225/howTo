Breakfast

Classic American Breakfast

Ingredients

Pancakes

  • 1½ cups flour
  • 3½ teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 tablespoon white sugar
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • 1¼ cups milk
  • 3 tablespoons butter, melted
  • 1 large egg

Bacon

  • 1 (16 ounce) package thick-cut bacon

Sunny-side Up Eggs

  • 1 teaspoon beef tallow (or your choice of cooking oil)
  • 2 large eggs
  • salt and black pepper

Directions

Pancakes

  1. Mix flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt together in a large bowl. Make a well in the center and add milk, melted butter, and egg. Mix until smooth.
  2. Heat a lightly oiled griddle/pan over medium-high heat. Pour about 1/4 cup of batter onto griddle; cook until bubbles form. Flip and repeat.

Bacon

  1. Line a large baking sheet with aluminum foil
  2. Arrange bacon strips 1/2-inch apart on baking sheet. Place in cold oven
  3. Heat oven to 425 degrees F (220 degrees C). Cook bacon for 14 minutes.
  4. Transfer cooked bacon to paper towel-lined plate. Let cool for 5 minutes.

Sunny-side Up Eggs

  1. Heat tallow/oil in medium cast iron skillet for about 5 minutes.
  2. Crack an egg in a small bowl and slowly add to skillet. Repeat with other egg.
  3. Cover with lid and cook until whites are completely set, but yolk is still runny.
  4. Once done, slide eggs out of skillet and onto plate. Season with salt and pepper.

Place all food onto plate and enjoy!

Lunch

Chicken, Bacon, & Avocado Wrap

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried basil
  • 2 teaspoons lemon juice
  • salt and black pepper to taste
  • 4-5 large tortillas
  • 1 avocado (diced)
  • 5-6 slices bacon (cooked & chopped)
  • 1 large cooked chicken breast (shredded)

Directions

  1. In a small bowl, combine mayonnaise, dried basil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  2. In a large bowl, toss together shredded lettuce, avocado, bacon, and chicken.
  3. Add mayonnaise mixture to the large bowl and toss to coat evenly. You may not need all of the dressing.
  4. Top tortillas with a large spoonful of the filling. Roll-up each wrap and secure with a toothpick if necessary.

Put tortilla wraps on plate and enjoy!

Dessert

Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients

  • 2 ¼ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup (2 sticks) butter, softened
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 3/4 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 cups semi-sweet chocolate chips

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees F (191 degrees C)
  2. Combine flour, baking soda, and salt in a small bowl. Set aside. Add eggs and beat well.
  3. Beat butter, sugar, brown sugar, and vanilla extract in a large mixer bowl until creamy.
  4. Gradually beat in flour mixture. Stir in chocolate chips.
  5. Drop by tablespoon onto ungreased baking sheets.
  6. Bake for 9 to 11 minutes or until golden brown. Cool on baking sheet for 2 mintues.

Serve warm with milk and enjoy!
